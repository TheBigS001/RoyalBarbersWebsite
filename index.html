The shop

Royal Barbers is a barbershop at 323A Haydons Road in Wimbledon, open seven days a week. We do haircuts, skin fades, beard trims, hot towel shaves and a few extras.

There is nothing to book. Come in when it suits you and you will be seen in turn. There is free Wi-Fi while you wait, and we sell hair products.

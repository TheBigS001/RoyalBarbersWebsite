The shop

Royal Barbers is on Haydons Road in Wimbledon. Walk-ins are welcome, and customers are seen in order of arrival.

We cut hair, do skin fades and beard trims, and offer hot towel shaves and a few extras. The full list is below. There is free Wi-Fi if you are waiting, and we sell hair products.

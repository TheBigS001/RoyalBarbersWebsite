Walk in, sit down, walk out sharp

Royal Barbers is a barbershop at 323A Haydons Road in Wimbledon. Come in when you like and you will be seen in turn, or book a slot and skip the wait.

From a quick all over one grade to a skin fade with a full beard trim and shape up, the price list is below. We also sell hair products, and the Wi-Fi is free while you wait.
